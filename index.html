A fitness app for beginners, and a protein tracker

Ascend Fitness & Nutrition is an iPhone app that keeps workouts and food in one place. It is for someone starting from zero, and for anyone who wants the week planned without juggling separate apps.

Workouts are tailored to your goal, the equipment you have, how many days you can train, and how long you want to be there. Every exercise has a demo video. If the day falls apart, you can shorten it, move it, or train at home with no gym.

On the food side it is a protein tracker as well as a calorie log. Protein, carbs, and fat update against targets built from your body and your goal. Type a meal, scan a barcode, or take a photo of your plate and fix the amounts before you save.

Eating out stays in the same plan. Ascend ranks menu items at 137 restaurant chains — more than 3,000 items — so higher-protein options for your goal rise to the top. That includes places you already go, such as Chipotle, Chick-fil-A, CAVA, Shake Shack, Taco Bell, Sweetgreen, Starbucks, Dunkin', and In-N-Out.

If you are new to this, start with how to start working out as a complete beginner, then how much protein you actually need, and how to eat at restaurants without losing the week. Published protein numbers for three chains are on the restaurant guides. More notes live on the blog.

Questions people ask before downloading

What is Ascend Fitness & Nutrition?

Ascend Fitness & Nutrition is an iPhone workout and nutrition app. It builds workouts around your goal, equipment, days, and session length, tracks protein, carbs, fat, and calories, and ranks restaurant menus for your goal.

Is Ascend a good fitness app for beginners?

Yes. You can start with the days and session length you actually have, including short workouts at home with no gym. The beginner guide on this site walks through a first week. Ascend is also there if you already train.

Is Ascend a protein tracker?

Yes. The log shows protein, carbs, and fat against targets built from your body and your goal, along with calories. You can type a meal, scan a barcode, or snap a photo and adjust the amounts before you save.

Can I scan a photo of my food?

Yes. Snap a meal from the log, check the estimate, and change anything that looks off before it is saved.

Does Ascend show high-protein restaurant options?

Yes. Eating out covers 137 restaurant chains and more than 3,000 menu items, ranked so higher-protein options for your goal sit toward the top. Chains include Chipotle, Chick-fil-A, CAVA, Shake Shack, Taco Bell, Sweetgreen, and In-N-Out. Sample numbers from the chains' own nutrition info are published here.

How much does Ascend cost?

Ascend is free to download on the App Store. All Access is $14.99 a month or $74.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial.
