How it works

Your plan changes when your life does.

Most apps hand you a week and hope you follow it. Ascend watches what actually happens and rebuilds the week around it — so the plan you see is always the plan you can do.

When you miss a workoutThe session moves, the week stays balancedNothing piles up. The split re-spaces itself so muscles still recover. When your schedule changesPick new training days, keep your progressEdit Days rewrites which weekdays you train without resetting the plan. When recovery changesHard days land when you're readySmart swaps point today's work at the muscles that are actually fresh. When your equipment changesSwap any exercise, mid-workoutEvery alternative already fits what you have — and the demo clip follows. When you only have 20 minutesEmergency Exercise compresses today's planTell it how long you have. It keeps the highest-value lifts.
