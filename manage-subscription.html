Manage Your Subscription

Last updated: August 7, 2026

All Ascend subscriptions are billed through the App Store (Apple). That means your plan, billing, and cancellation are managed securely by Apple — Ascend never stores your payment details. Here's how to do everything.

Switch Plans

Want to change from weekly to monthly or annual, or move between plans? You can switch anytime:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap Ascend
  5. Choose a different plan under Subscription Options

Your new plan starts at the end of your current billing period. You can also switch right inside the app: Profile → My Membership → Manage Subscription → Choose Another Plan.

Cancel Your Subscription

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap Ascend
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

Your access continues until the end of your current billing period, and you won't be charged again. Your plans, logs, and progress stay saved to your account — if you come back, everything is right where you left it.

Request a Refund

Refunds for App Store subscriptions are handled directly by Apple:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID
  3. Find your Ascend purchase
  4. Select Request a refund

Free Trials

If you're on a free trial and cancel before it ends, you won't be charged at all. To avoid a charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Deleting the Ascend app does not cancel your subscription — subscriptions can only be cancelled through the steps above.

Still Need Help?

If you're having trouble or aren't sure about anything, email us at support@ascendfitness.ai and we'll help you out — usually within a day.
