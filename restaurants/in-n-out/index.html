High-protein options at In-N-Out

In-N-Out’s menu is short, and the nutrition page is specific. The Double-Double is the high-protein burger. Protein Style cuts calories by swapping the bun for lettuce, and it also cuts a few grams of protein. A shake can match the burger’s calories with far more sugar.

What the sheet is saying

A hamburger is 16 grams of protein and 360 calories. Asking for mustard and ketchup instead of spread keeps the 16 grams and drops the burger to 300 calories. That is a real, small change. Protein Style drops the same hamburger to 210 calories and 12 grams of protein. The lettuce wrap is the lower-calorie burger, not the higher-protein one. The bun was part of the protein count.

Cheese moves a hamburger to a cheeseburger: 20 grams of protein and 430 calories, or 16 grams and 280 calories Protein Style. The Double-Double is the clear protein order on this menu, at 34 grams and 610 calories, or 30 grams and 460 calories without the bun. If you want the most protein In-N-Out lists on these rows, order the Double-Double and decide separately whether the bun is worth 4 grams of protein and 150 calories.

Fries are 360 calories and 6 grams of protein, about the same calories as the hamburger and less than half the protein. The 15-ounce chocolate shake is 610 calories and 16 grams of protein, with 61 grams of sugars on the same page. That is the same calorie number as the Double-Double, with less than half the protein and a dessert’s worth of sugar. Order it as dessert if you want it. Do not think of it as the protein part of the meal, even though milk adds some protein.

A straightforward order

For a higher-protein meal: Double-Double, or Double-Double Protein Style if you want fewer carbohydrates. Water instead of a shake. Fries only if you still want them after looking at 360 calories and 6 grams of protein. For a smaller meal: hamburger with mustard and ketchup instead of spread, 16 grams of protein and 300 calories.

Questions

Where do these In-N-Out numbers come from?

They come from In-N-Out’s nutrition info page at in-n-out.com/menu/nutrition-info, accessed October 27, 2026. The hamburger, cheeseburger, and Double-Double figures below are the standard “with onion” items as listed, before the mustard-and-ketchup variation.

Does Protein Style add protein?

No. On that page, Protein Style means the bun is replaced with lettuce. The hamburger goes from 16 grams of protein and 360 calories to 12 grams and 210 calories. The Double-Double goes from 34 grams and 610 calories to 30 grams and 460 calories. You drop more calories than protein, and you do lose some protein.
