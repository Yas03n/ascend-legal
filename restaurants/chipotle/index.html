High-protein options at Chipotle

Chipotle prints nutrition by ingredient, so you can add a bowl instead of guessing. These figures are from the company’s U.S. nutrition PDF, not from a third-party calculator. A higher-protein bowl is mostly a protein serving plus beans, not a larger bag of chips.

Source: Chipotle U.S. Nutrition Facts paper menu (March 2025 PDF), accessed October 27, 2026. Standard portions are the ones in that file. Restaurant scoops vary.

One way to add a bowl

Using only those published portions: chicken 32 g, black beans 8 g, fajita vegetables 1 g, and supergreens 1 g. That is 42 grams of protein. Calories: 180 + 130 + 20 + 15 = 345. White rice would add 4 grams of protein and 210 calories. A regular order of chips would add 7 grams of protein and 540 calories, which is more food than the bowl itself.

Sofritas are the plant option on this sheet, at 8 grams of protein per 4 ounces, the same published protein as a portion of beans and far less than chicken. If you want a higher plant-based number, the sheet supports beans plus sofritas, not sofritas alone. Cheese adds 6 grams of protein and 110 calories per ounce. Guacamole adds mostly calories (230) and 2 grams of protein per 4 ounces. Order it because you want it, and count it.

How to order it

Ask for a bowl, not a burrito, if you want to skip the 320-calorie tortilla. Pick chicken, barbacoa, carnitas, or steak as the center. Add beans if you want another 8 grams. Add fajita vegetables or supergreens because they barely move calories. Decide on rice, cheese, guacamole, and chips as separate yes-or-no items. Salsa on the PDF is low in protein. It is there for taste.

Questions

Where do these Chipotle numbers come from?

They come from Chipotle’s U.S. Nutrition Facts paper menu PDF dated March 2025, hosted on chipotle.com. We read it on October 27, 2026. Portions are the ones printed on that sheet, such as 4 ounces of chicken.

Will my bowl match the total exactly?

Not always. A scoop in the restaurant can be larger or smaller than the published portion, and recipes change. Use the sheet as a planning guide, then adjust if your portion was obviously different.
