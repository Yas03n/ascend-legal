High-protein options at Chick-fil-A

Chick-fil-A publishes calories and protein for each standard item. Grilled nuggets are the simplest high-protein, lower-calorie order on the sheet we read. A club sandwich has more protein and also a lot more calories. Both can fit. They fit different days.

Source: Chick-fil-A nutrition and allergens guide, accessed October 27, 2026. Formulas change. Check the guide again if you are ordering from a newer menu.

How to read that table

Eight grilled nuggets give 25 grams of protein for 130 calories. That is an easy add next to a side you actually like, or a full snack if you are between meals. Twelve nuggets move the same idea to 38 grams and 200 calories. The Grilled Chicken Sandwich lands at 28 grams and 390 calories, bun included. The club goes to 37 grams and 520 calories. You pay for the extra protein with cheese, bacon, and calories. If you only needed the protein, the 12-count nuggets publish more protein than the sandwich at about half the calories.

The standard Cobb salad is the highest protein row here, at 41 grams, and also 830 calories. The warm grilled-filet version of the Cobb is 35 grams and 600 calories on the same guide. A salad is not automatically the lighter order. Read the line.

Breakfast has a straightforward option: the Egg White Grill is 27 grams of protein and 300 calories as listed. That is a complete morning item without a shake.

What this page leaves out

Sauces, fries, lemonade, and milkshakes are on Chick-fil-A’s full guide and they change the meal. This page does not reprint the whole guide. If you add a sauce, look up that sauce. The entree numbers above are for the item as named.

Questions

Where do these Chick-fil-A numbers come from?

They come from Chick-fil-A’s nutrition and allergens guide at chick-fil-a.com, accessed October 27, 2026. Each row below is the standard item as listed there, not a customized sandwich.

Is the highest-protein item always the best order?

No. On that same guide, the Cobb salad is 41 grams of protein and 830 calories, while 8-count Grilled Nuggets are 25 grams of protein and 130 calories. Protein per calorie is very different. Pick the item that fits the rest of the day.

Does Ascend include Chick-fil-A?

Yes. Chick-fil-A is one of the 137 chains in Ascend. The app ranks the menu for your goal. This page only quotes a few official rows.
